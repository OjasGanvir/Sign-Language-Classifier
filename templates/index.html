PROJECT INFORMATION

Gesture Classifier

A webcam-based prototype that recognizes hand gestures for letters and displays the result on screen.

How it works

  1. The webcam captures a hand in view.
  2. MediaPipe detects key points, called landmarks, on the hand.
  3. A trained machine-learning model uses those landmarks to predict a gesture.
  4. The predicted letter appears on the camera screen.

How to try it

  • Allow camera access and keep one hand clearly inside the camera frame.
  • Use a well-lit space and hold your hand where the camera can see it.
  • Make a hand gesture and watch the detected letter on the screen.
  • J and Z use movement gestures; trace the letter shape with your hand.

What this project recognizes

The model classifies hand gestures as letters. This is a gesture and alphabet-letter recognition prototype, not a translator for complete sign-language conversations.

Limitations

Lighting, camera quality, hand position, distance from the camera, and differences between users can affect recognition. The project is a learning demonstration and may not recognize every gesture correctly.
